# SkyLine Airways — Airline Policies

---

## Cancellation Policy

**Within 24 hours of booking (Risk-Free Window)**
Customers may cancel any booking within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund, regardless of fare type, as long as the flight departs at least 7 days from the booking date.

**Standard Fares (Economy)**
- Cancellations made 7+ days before departure: $75 cancellation fee; remaining balance refunded as travel credit valid for 12 months.
- Cancellations made 3–6 days before departure: $150 fee; remainder as travel credit.
- Cancellations within 72 hours of departure: non-refundable; travel credit of 50% of ticket value.

**Flexible Fares (Economy Flex / Business)**
- Cancellations at any time before departure: full refund to original payment method, no fee.

**No-Show Policy**
Passengers who do not cancel and do not board forfeit the full ticket value with no credit issued.

---

## Flight Change Policy

**Standard Fares**
- Changes made 7+ days before departure: $50 change fee + any fare difference.
- Changes made 3–6 days before departure: $100 change fee + fare difference.
- Changes within 72 hours of departure: $150 change fee + fare difference.
- Same-day changes (within 24 hours of departure): subject to availability; $200 fee.

**Flexible Fares**
- Unlimited free changes up to 2 hours before departure; only fare difference applies.

**How to Change**
Call 1-800-SKY-LINE or visit skylineairways.com/manage. Changes cannot be made at the gate.

---

## Refund Policy

- Refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method (credit/debit card).
- Travel credits are issued within 24 hours and are valid for 12 months from the issue date.
- Travel credits are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Flight disruptions caused by SkyLine Airways (delays over 3 hours, cancellations): full refund to original payment method, no fee, regardless of fare type.

---

## Baggage Policy

**Carry-On**
- 1 carry-on bag (max 22" × 14" × 9", max 15 lbs) — included on all fares.
- 1 personal item (purse, laptop bag, small backpack) — included on all fares.

**Checked Baggage**
- First checked bag: $35 (Standard) / Free (Flexible & Business)
- Second checked bag: $50 (all fares)
- Oversize bags (over 62 linear inches): $100 additional fee
- Overweight bags (51–70 lbs): $75 fee; bags over 70 lbs are not accepted.

**Sports Equipment**
Bikes, skis, and golf clubs: $75 each way, pre-booked. Contact us in advance for surfboards or other large items.

---

## Seat Selection Policy

- Standard Fares: seat selection available from $10–$45 depending on seat location. Unselected seats are assigned at check-in at no charge.
- Flexible Fares: free standard seat selection included; exit row and premium seats from $20.
- Business Class: all seats included at no extra charge.
- Families with children under 12 are automatically seated together at no charge where possible.

---

## Check-In Policy

- Online / App check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 1 hour before departure.
- Airport check-in counter closes 45 minutes before domestic departure, 60 minutes before international.
- Boarding gates close 15 minutes before departure — passengers not at the gate by then may be denied boarding without refund.

---

## Special Assistance

Passengers requiring wheelchair assistance, traveling with medical equipment, or needing other accommodations should contact SkyLine Airways at least 48 hours before departure at 1-800-SKY-LINE or accessibility@skylineairways.com.
